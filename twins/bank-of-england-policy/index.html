Limitations

What this model cannot show

  • This preview is a partial digital twin, not a complete representation of the Bank of England or UK economy.
  • CPIH is a broad ONS consumer-price measure and is not the Bank of England's operational CPI target measure.
  • The Labour Force Survey estimate is subject to sampling variability and revision and should be read alongside other labour indicators.
  • Quarterly GDP estimates can be revised and do not measure GDP per head, household welfare or monthly demand conditions.
  • Conceptual relationships describe mechanisms but do not claim current causal magnitudes.
  • The next-decision distribution is an external participant survey rather than a proprietary MGI forecast.
  • The twin provides research and education, not investment advice or a trading signal.
