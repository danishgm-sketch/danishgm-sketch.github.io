What MGI is today

MGI Pulse is a working public beta. It tracks four UK indicators, records forecasts before outcomes, and publishes its sources and limits. Many planned MGI products do not exist yet.

Implemented

  • Official Bank Rate ingestion and daily source validation
  • Official ONS CPIH annual rate ingestion for L55O/MM23
  • Official ONS unemployment-rate ingestion for MGSX/LMS
  • Official ONS real GDP quarter-on-quarter ingestion for IHYQ/QNA
  • Independent source-health states and safe value withholding
  • One validated source registry and public official source register
  • Shared fail-closed source retrieval and opaque request tracing
  • Optional PostgreSQL source, ingestion and observation foundation
  • Deterministic four-series migration and fixture-seed lifecycle
  • Append-only observation revisions and historical knowledge-cutoff queries
  • Reproducible Market Pulse snapshot with exact observation and source versions
  • Private checksum-verified archive for approved source artefacts
  • Deterministic policy-rate and CPIH inflation states
  • Quality-aware unemployment trend using non-overlapping periods
  • Four-state Market Pulse with distinct time semantics
  • Immutable Daily Brief and evidence manifest
  • Release capability and limitation history
  • Resolved monetary-policy event with expectation and surprise
  • Immutable open forecast published before outcome
  • Precommitted scoring and guarded resolution workflow
  • Versioned Digital Twin Studio registry
  • Policy twin with rate, inflation, labour and growth state
  • Point-in-time policy-rate checkpoints
  • Decision vote margin and rate-surprise state
  • Forecast entropy and concentration metrics
  • Connected-state and missing-coverage map
  • Conceptual mechanism map with explicit evidence limits
  • Permanent disclaimer, privacy, terms, corrections and contact routes
  • Public GitHub correction and contact forms
  • Validated append-only correction register
  • Distinctive responsive public-beta identity and live-signal homepage
  • Public launch-readiness and canonical-domain record

Not yet implemented

  • Authoritative outcome for the 30 July 2026 decision
  • Final selected Brier score and published error analysis
  • Policy-transmission twin state
  • Wages, vacancies, inactivity and employment triangulation
  • Canonical CPI target and inflation-component decomposition
  • Production database deployment and persistence for events, forecasts, publications and twins
  • Automatic archiving of every live source response and production object storage
  • Proprietary MGI forecasts, scenarios and accounts
  • Grounded Ask MGI and commercial products
  • Professional legal and privacy review
