Privacy notice · effective 2026-07-29

What data MGI handles today

MGI is currently a static public website. The application does not provide accounts, first-party forms, analytics, advertising trackers or marketing profiles.

Who is responsible

MGI project operator, GitHub account @danishgm-sketch is the current operational contact for information submitted through the MGI public feedback repository. MGI has not yet published a separate incorporated legal operator or branded email address. That identity and contact disclosure is a recorded gate before broad commercial promotion.

Website visits

MGI is currently configured for GitHub Pages. MGI code does not set cookies or use local storage, session storage, tracking pixels, device fingerprinting or first-party analytics. A consent banner would therefore have nothing for a visitor to accept or reject. This position must be reviewed before any storage or access technology is added.

GitHub states that it logs visitor IP addresses for security when a GitHub Pages site is visited. GitHub processes hosting data under its own privacy statement. The MGI application does not receive those IP logs.

  • How GitHub Pages handles visits
  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • ICO guidance on cookies and similar technologies

Feedback and correction reports

The contact and correction links open public GitHub issue forms. GitHub records the account name, issue content, timestamps and related activity. Submissions are public. Do not include passwords, private financial information, identity documents, health information or other sensitive personal data.

MGI uses submissions to investigate corrections, answer product questions, prevent abuse and maintain a public accountability record. The intended basis is the legitimate interest in operating and improving a public research service and, where applicable, meeting legal obligations.

Who receives the data

Public issue content can be read by anyone. GitHub hosts and processes the repository. MGI does not sell feedback data, use it for advertising or feed it into a public model-training system.

Retention

Accepted correction records are kept as part of the permanent publication history. Other public issues may remain available while they are useful to the product record. Spam, abusive material, accidentally disclosed sensitive data or content that must be removed for safety or legal reasons may be deleted or redacted. GitHub controls retention in its own systems.

Your choices and rights

You can avoid submitting information and still read MGI. Depending on the law that applies, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, and may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

If a privacy request needs private details, open only a minimal contact issue asking for a private reply route. Do not place the private details in the public issue. You can also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office.

External links

MGI links to official sources and other websites. Those services apply their own privacy practices when you leave MGI.

Review status

This notice records the application's current data footprint as reviewed on 2026-07-29. It has not received professional legal or privacy review. A hosting change, custom-domain activation, form, account, analytics tool, embedded media or other new processor requires a fresh inventory and notice version before release.
