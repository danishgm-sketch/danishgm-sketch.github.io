Privacy notice · effective 2026-08-01

Privacy notice

MGI is currently a static public website. The application does not provide accounts, first-party forms, analytics, advertising trackers or marketing profiles.

Who is responsible

MGI project operator, GitHub account @danishgm-sketch currently handles information sent through the public MGI feedback repository. MGI has not yet published a separate company identity or branded email address. Those details must be confirmed before wider promotion.

Website visits

OpenAI GPT Sites hosts this website. MGI does not set cookies or use browser storage, tracking pixels, device fingerprinting or its own analytics. This must be reviewed before any of those technologies are added.

OpenAI processes the data needed to host, maintain and support the site. The MGI application does not receive the host's security logs.

  • ChatGPT Sites Terms
  • ChatGPT Sites Data Processing Addendum
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • ICO guidance on cookies and similar technologies

Feedback and correction reports

The contact and correction links open public GitHub issue forms. GitHub records the account name, issue content, timestamps and related activity. Submissions are public. Do not include passwords, private financial information, identity documents, health information or other sensitive personal data.

MGI uses submissions to investigate corrections, answer questions, prevent abuse and keep a public record of accepted changes. The intended legal basis is the legitimate interest in running and improving a public research service and, where required, meeting legal obligations.

Who receives the data

Public issue content can be read by anyone. GitHub hosts and processes the repository. MGI does not sell feedback data, use it for advertising or feed it into a public model-training system.

Retention

Accepted correction records are kept as part of the permanent publication history. Other public issues may remain available while they are useful to the product record. Spam, abusive material, accidentally disclosed sensitive data or content that must be removed for safety or legal reasons may be deleted or redacted. GitHub controls retention in its own systems.

Your choices and rights

You can avoid submitting information and still read MGI. Depending on the law that applies, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, and may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

If a privacy request needs private details, open only a minimal contact issue asking for a private reply route. Do not place the private details in the public issue. You can also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office.

External links

MGI links to official sources and other websites. Those services apply their own privacy practices when you leave MGI.

Review status

This notice describes the website as reviewed on 2026-08-01. It has not received professional legal or privacy review. The notice must be updated before MGI changes host or adds forms, accounts, analytics, embedded media or another company that handles visitor data.
