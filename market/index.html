Quality boundary

Quarterly estimate, subject to revision

  • The registered value is real GDP growth measured using chained volume measures and is seasonally adjusted.
  • Quarter-on-quarter growth rates are published to one decimal place and may be revised when additional source data or seasonal-adjustment updates become available.
  • One quarterly estimate does not represent GDP per head, household welfare or the full path of demand.

MGI calls growth above 0.1% expanding and growth below −0.1% contracting. Values between those points are broadly flat. These are MGI display rules, not ONS labels.
