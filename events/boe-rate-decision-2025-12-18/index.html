Bank of England rate decision — December 2025

Bank of England Monetary Policy Committee reduced Bank Rate from 4.00% to 3.75%. The recorded rate outcome matched the survey median expectation.

Rate outcome matched expectation

3.75%

Actual Bank Rate

Published 18 Dec 2025 after the meeting ended 17 Dec 2025.

Expectation

3.75%

Median modal expectation from 82 respondents. The 25th and 75th percentiles were both 3.75%.

Survey window: 3 Dec 2025 to 5 Dec 2025.

Open expectation source

Outcome

3.75%

Bank Rate fell by 0.25 percentage points from the previous 4.00% level.

Open decision and minutes

Rate surprise

0.00 percentage points

actual Bank Rate minus median modal expectation. This compares the expected and actual rates only. It does not capture everything in the decision.

Vote split

5–4

5 members voted to reduce, 4 to maintain, and 0 to increase.

What the result means

Expected rate, contested decision

The rate matched the survey median, but the 5–4 vote shows that the committee was closely divided. This is a factual comparison, not a trading signal.

Timeline

From survey to official decision

  1. 3 Dec 2025

    Expectation survey opened

    The Bank's Market Participants Survey collected responses from 3 to 5 December 2025.

  2. 17 Dec 2025

    MPC meeting concluded

    The decision meeting ended with five members supporting a 0.25 percentage-point reduction and four preferring no change.

  3. 18 Dec 2025

    Decision published

    Bank Rate was reduced from 4.00% to 3.75%.

  4. 19 Dec 2025

    Survey results published

    The published survey confirmed a 3.75% median modal expectation from 82 respondents.
