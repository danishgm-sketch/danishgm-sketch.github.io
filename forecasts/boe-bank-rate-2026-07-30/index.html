Limits and invalidation

The rules cannot change after the result

  • The scheduled decision is cancelled or moved beyond the resolution deadline.
  • The Bank of England does not publish an authoritative Bank Rate outcome within 30 days.
  • The target definition becomes ambiguous because more than one official rate outcome applies to the same event.
  • This is a registered external survey baseline, not a proprietary MGI model forecast.
  • The probabilities are published survey means rounded to one decimal place and therefore total 99.9%, not exactly 100%.
  • The distribution concerns the Bank Rate level only and does not forecast the vote split, statement language, inflation projections or market reaction.
  • The record is general financial research and is not an investment recommendation or trading signal.
