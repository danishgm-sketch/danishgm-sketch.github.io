Research Disclaimer

MGI provides general financial research and education. It does not tell you what to trade.

What MGI provides

MGI explains official data, past events and forecasts using fixed rules. The material is written for a general audience and is not tailored to you. A clear explanation or prominent display is still not a recommendation.

What MGI does not provide

MGI does not provide personal investment, financial, legal, tax or accounting advice. It does not assess suitability, issue buy, sell or hold ratings, execute orders, connect to a broker, size positions or allocate a portfolio.

Forecasts and uncertainty

Forecasts can be wrong. MGI keeps the original probabilities, publishes the scoring rule before the result and leaves failed forecasts visible. A forecast or descriptive label is information, not an instruction to act.

Data and availability

Official statistics can be delayed, corrected or revised. MGI shows source health and known limits, but readers should check the linked authoritative source before relying on a figure. The site may be changed, interrupted or withdrawn.

Your decisions remain yours

You are responsible for decisions you make using public information. If you need advice specific to your position, use an appropriately authorised professional who can assess your circumstances.

Review status

This implementation draft describes the current product boundary. It has not received specialist UK financial-services legal review and does not claim FCA approval, authorisation or legal clearance.
