03 / METHOD AND LIMITATIONS

Method and limitations

How the publication is maintained

  1. 01
    Named sources

    Each figure names the Bank of England or ONS series behind it.

  2. 02
    No guessed figures

    If both the live source and verified saved copy fail, MGI shows no value.

  3. 03
    Old claims stay visible

    Forecasts, releases, limits and corrections remain available after updates.

Scope

Research and education only

MGI provides general financial and economic information. It offers no personal recommendations, buy or sell ratings, broker links, position sizes or portfolio instructions.
