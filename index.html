01 / UK INDICATORS

Different measures, different periods

Four figures. Four different clocks.

Bank Rate can change on a single day. Inflation refers to a month. Unemployment covers three months. GDP covers a quarter. MGI keeps those periods separate, so the latest number does not lose its meaning.

03 / HOW THE RECORD WORKS

Evidence, expectation, outcome

Each update keeps its place in time.

  1. 01
    What was known

    MGI records the source, figure, period and information available at publication.

  2. 02
    What was expected

    Published forecasts keep their original probabilities and scoring rules.

  3. 03
    What happened

    Official outcomes are added from named sources. Earlier claims remain visible.

  4. 04
    What changed later

    Revisions and corrections create a new record without silently rewriting the old one.
