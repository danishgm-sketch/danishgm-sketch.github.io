Corrections

Material corrections create a new publication version and preserve the original record. Forecast probabilities are not changed after an outcome becomes knowable.

Report a possible error

Use the public correction form and include the page, disputed statement, supporting evidence and the change you think is needed. The submission and discussion are public.

Open the correction form

Do not include private financial information, identity documents, passwords or other sensitive data.

What happens next

  1. MGI records and checks the report against authoritative evidence.
  2. The report is accepted, rejected or left open with a reason.
  3. An accepted material correction receives a correction ID and a new publication version.
  4. The register states what changed, why it changed and where the original can still be inspected.
