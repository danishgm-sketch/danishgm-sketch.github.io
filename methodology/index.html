Methodology · version pulse-rules-0.6.11

How MGI Pulse 0.6.11 works

The release is deterministic, multi-series, event-aware, forecast-accountable and twin-aware. It does not use a language model to decide policy, inflation, labour or growth states, surprises, forecast probabilities, outcomes, scores or twin coverage.

1. Official sources

The Market Pulse uses Bank of England Official Bank Rate series IUDBEDR, ONS CPIH annual rate series L55O in dataset MM23, ONS unemployment-rate series MGSX in dataset LMS and ONS real GDP quarter-on-quarter series IHYQ in dataset QNA.

All four adapters resolve one versioned registry. Each record fixes the source identity, HTTPS access route, parser, measurement, rights, attribution, cost, planned request cap, repository fallback, refresh thresholds and review date. An unknown provider-published rate limit remains explicitly unknown rather than being invented. Duplicate identities, non-HTTPS URLs, escaped fallback paths and incomplete rights fail validation.

2. Common live-source safety

Every adapter uses the same fail-closed retrieval layer. A request must use HTTPS, the exact approved host and registered path, no credentials, no non-standard port, no fragment and no redirect. The layer sends a common research-and-education user agent, applies a ten-second timeout, validates the declared content type and enforces each source's byte limit while streaming the body.

Only a timeout, network failure, HTTP 408, HTTP 429 or server error can receive one bounded retry. Unsafe routes, unexpected content, oversized bodies and parser failures are not retried. Failures have a typed category and opaque request trace; diagnostic messages omit the request URL, query values, credentials, parser error text and response body.

3. CPIH scope

CPIH includes owner-occupiers’ housing costs. MGI stores the reference month separately from the ONS publication and verification dates. CPIH is a broad descriptive inflation dimension and is not substituted for the Bank of England's operational CPI target measure.

4. CPIH state and trend

The latest annual rate is compared with the preceding reference month. A negative change is falling, a positive change rising and a negligible change unchanged. Descriptive levels are low at 1% or below, moderate above 1% through 3%, elevated above 3% through 5%, and high above 5%.

5. Unemployment scope

The labour state uses the seasonally adjusted unemployment rate for people aged 16 and over. Each observation is a three-month Labour Force Survey estimate rather than an exact administrative count.

6. Non-overlapping labour comparison

The latest March–May 2026 estimate is compared with December 2025–February 2026. MGI deliberately avoids adjacent overlapping three-month windows for the headline comparison.

7. Labour trend and quality rule

A change of at least 0.20 percentage points is labelled higher or lower. A smaller movement is labelled broadly stable. This is an MGI interface rule, not an official ONS classification.

The ONS quality warning remains visible: Labour Force Survey estimates are subject to sampling variability, short-term volatility and later revision and should be considered alongside other labour-market indicators.

8. Real GDP scope

The growth state uses the ONS seasonally adjusted chained-volume measure of real GDP growth from the preceding quarter. The series reports a quarterly percentage change. It does not report an annual rate, monthly nowcast, GDP per head or household welfare.

9. Growth state and momentum

Quarterly growth above 0.1% is labelled expanding, below −0.1% contracting and the inclusive range between them broadly flat. A change in the quarterly rate above 0.1 percentage points is strengthening, below −0.1 weakening and otherwise little changed. MGI defines these interface rules; ONS does not publish them as classifications.

10. GDP revision rule

ONS quarterly estimates are rounded to one decimal place and can be revised as more evidence and seasonal-adjustment updates become available. MGI records the release vintage and does not present a rounded estimate as false precision.

11. Independent source health

Bank Rate, CPIH, unemployment and real GDP growth are fetched and validated independently. Each has its own live, verified-fallback, aging, stale and unavailable state. Failure of one source does not suppress valid states from the others.

12. Validation and time

Observation, reference-period, verification, refresh, survey, publication, target and resolution dates remain separate. External payloads and repository fixtures are schema-validated before publication.

13. Source history

Bank Rate is a change-point series and may be carried forward to historical target dates with disclosure. CPIH remains monthly. Unemployment remains a rolling three-month estimate and is not relabelled as a single-month observation. Real GDP growth remains quarterly and tied to its registered release vintage.

14. Immutable publication and events

Daily Brief wording and inputs do not silently change. Resolved events preserve expectation, outcome, surprise, vote, evidence and timeline.

15. Forecast origin and distribution

The first open forecast registers the Bank of England June 2026 Market Participants Survey as an external baseline, not a proprietary MGI forecast. Its rounded categorical probabilities remain exactly as published.

16. Resolution and scoring

The authority, target, deadline and invalidation conditions are fixed before the outcome. Policy multiclass-brier-v1 normalises rounded percentages for scoring only and applies the multiclass Brier score sum((p_i - o_i)^2). Lower is better.

17. Guarded resolution operation

The resolver accepts only an open forecast, a registered outcome and target-date-or-later authoritative evidence. It preserves the original distribution and opens a reviewed pull request rather than merging automatically.

18. Digital twin definition

The first policy twin connects verified Bank Rate, CPIH, unemployment and real GDP growth state, a represented decision and the next-decision expectation distribution. Policy transmission remains an explicit gap.

19. Relationship status

Inflation-to-policy and labour-to-policy links remain conceptual. MGI does not infer current causal magnitude, direction or lag from headline observations alone.

20. Forecast uncertainty metric

Shannon entropy is calculated from the complete normalised categorical distribution. It describes distribution concentration, not guaranteed confidence.

21. Publication revision rule

Forecast probabilities, earlier publications and historical checkpoints are not silently rewritten. Later ONS revisions create new source versions rather than altering archived evidence.

22. Persistence foundation

The optional PostgreSQL schema stores registered source identity, ingestion attempts and exact-decimal observations. Observation validity, publication, retrieval, verification, ingestion and knowledge-availability times are separate UTC fields. Migrations are owned by a dedicated role; the application role cannot create schema objects or update an observation after insertion.

The current public website remains repository-backed and performs no database request per page view. No production database has been provisioned. A failed or absent database therefore cannot invent a public value or take the static site offline.

23. Product boundary

MGI provides general financial research and education. Market states, forecasts, scores, twins and mechanism maps are not personal recommendations, trade signals or instructions.

24. Correction rule

A material correction receives its own ID and corrected publication version. The register records what changed, why it changed and where the original can still be inspected. An empty register is shown as empty rather than treated as proof that no errors exist.

25. Governance and data footprint

The permanent disclaimer, privacy, terms, corrections and contact routes are generated from a validated governance record. The current static application has no accounts, first-party analytics, marketing trackers, browser storage, first-party forms or non-essential cookies. GitHub hosts the site and the public feedback repository.

These governance pages are implementation drafts. They have not received professional legal review.

26. Public interface and domain state

The homepage reads the same four validated datasets as Market Pulse and applies no new numerical interpretation. Its signal field is a presentation layer over existing deterministic states. The visual system does not use colour as the only status indicator and respects reduced-motion preferences.

marketgeneralintelligence.co.uk is the intended canonical domain and marketgeneralintelligence.com is reserved for a route-preserving redirect. Neither is described as active until DNS, HTTPS and redirects are verified. The current metadata falls back to the recorded GitHub Pages address unless a validated deployment provides NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL.
