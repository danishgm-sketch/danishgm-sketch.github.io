Methodology · version pulse-rules-0.6.15

How MGI Pulse 0.6.15 works

MGI uses fixed rules, not AI-generated judgement, to label the four economic indicators and calculate forecast scores. This page publishes those rules.

1. Official sources

The figures come from four named series: Bank of England Bank Rate IUDBEDR, ONS CPIH inflation L55O, ONS unemployment MGSX and ONS quarterly real GDP growth IHYQ.

MGI keeps one checked record for each source. It includes the web address, measurement, licence, attribution, cost, saved copy, update schedule and review date. Missing information stays marked as unknown. Invalid or incomplete records are rejected.

2. Checks for live data

MGI downloads data only over HTTPS from the registered address. It rejects redirects, unexpected file types and oversized responses, and stops a request after ten seconds.

A temporary network or provider error gets one retry. Unsafe addresses, malformed data and oversized files do not. Error records exclude credentials, response bodies and other sensitive request details.

3. CPIH scope

CPIH includes owner-occupiers’ housing costs. MGI stores the reference month separately from the ONS publication and verification dates. CPIH is a broad descriptive inflation dimension and is not substituted for the Bank of England's operational CPI target measure.

4. CPIH state and trend

The latest annual rate is compared with the preceding reference month. A negative change is falling, a positive change rising and a negligible change unchanged. Descriptive levels are low at 1% or below, moderate above 1% through 3%, elevated above 3% through 5%, and high above 5%.

5. Unemployment scope

The unemployment figure covers people aged 16 and over and is adjusted for normal seasonal patterns. It is a three-month survey estimate, not an exact count.

6. Non-overlapping labour comparison

The March to May 2026 estimate is compared with December 2025 to February 2026. The two periods do not share any months.

7. Labour trend and quality rule

A change of at least 0.20 percentage points is labelled higher or lower. A smaller movement is labelled broadly stable. This is an MGI interface rule, not an official ONS classification.

The ONS quality warning remains visible: Labour Force Survey estimates are subject to sampling variability, short-term volatility and later revision and should be considered alongside other labour-market indicators.

8. Real GDP scope

The growth figure is the change in inflation-adjusted UK GDP from the previous quarter. It is not an annual rate, monthly estimate, GDP per person or measure of household wellbeing.

9. Growth state and momentum

Quarterly growth above 0.1% is labelled expanding, below −0.1% contracting and the inclusive range between them broadly flat. A change in the quarterly rate above 0.1 percentage points is strengthening, below −0.1 weakening and otherwise little changed. MGI defines these interface rules; ONS does not publish them as classifications.

10. GDP revision rule

ONS quarterly estimates are rounded to one decimal place and can be revised as more evidence and seasonal-adjustment updates become available. MGI records the release vintage and does not present a rounded estimate as false precision.

11. Independent source health

MGI checks each of the four sources separately. A figure may be live, taken from a verified saved copy, out of date or unavailable. One failed source does not hide the others.

12. Validation and time

MGI stores dates for the period measured, publication, retrieval and verification separately. Forecasts also keep their survey, target and resolution dates. All downloaded data and saved copies must pass format checks before publication.

13. Source history

Bank Rate is a change-point series and may be carried forward to historical target dates with disclosure. CPIH remains monthly. Unemployment remains a rolling three-month estimate and is not relabelled as a single-month observation. Real GDP growth remains quarterly and tied to its registered release vintage.

14. Immutable publication and events

A published Daily Brief keeps its original wording and inputs. A completed event keeps the expectation, result, vote, sources and timeline.

15. Forecast origin and distribution

The open forecast uses the Bank of England's June 2026 Market Participants Survey. It is not an MGI model forecast. The published probabilities remain unchanged.

16. Resolution and scoring

MGI fixed the source, target, deadline and invalidation rules before the result. Policy multiclass-brier-v1 adjusts the rounded percentages for scoring only, then applies the multiclass Brier score sum((p_i - o_i)^2). A lower score is better.

17. Guarded resolution operation

The forecast can be resolved only with an allowed outcome and official evidence published on or after the target date. The original probabilities cannot change, and the result requires review before publication.

18. Digital twin definition

The Bank of England policy model brings together Bank Rate, inflation, unemployment, GDP growth, a past decision and the next-decision forecast. It does not yet model how policy changes affect the economy.

19. Relationship status

The links between inflation, employment and Bank Rate are explanatory only. Four headline figures are not enough to measure the size, direction or timing of cause and effect.

20. Forecast uncertainty metric

MGI calculates Shannon entropy from all forecast probabilities. It shows how concentrated the distribution is; it does not prove that the forecast is reliable.

21. Observation vintages and historical cutoffs

The local database stores each later official value as a new observation linked to the exact record it replaces. It never updates the earlier value. A historical query returns the newest observation whose knowledge time is on or before the requested cutoff, so a later revision cannot leak into an earlier forecast or publication.

Three public labels are allowed. “First published” requires evidence that the archive starts with the first release. “Revised” requires two or more linked releases. “No earlier comparable vintage held” means MGI cannot tell from its records whether the value changed. That third label applies to the current public indicator files.

22. Publication revision rule

Forecast probabilities, earlier publications and historical checkpoints are not silently rewritten. Later ONS revisions create new source versions rather than altering archived evidence.

23. Persistence foundation

The optional PostgreSQL design stores source details, download attempts and exact figures. It keeps the relevant dates separate and prevents the website account from changing an observation after it is stored.

The public website reads versioned repository files and makes no database request when you open a page. No production database exists yet.

24. Private source archive

The local archive gives each approved source file an identity derived from its SHA-256 checksum. A separate record keeps the source, parser, file type, retrieval and verification times, licence, attribution and retention rule. Any change to the file or record fails verification.

Files are stored only when the source register permits storage. The archive refuses public website and build folders. The current command archives the four checked fallback files; it does not claim that those files are raw upstream responses or that cloud storage is connected.

25. Reproducible Market Pulse snapshot

Release 0.6.15 records the exact nine observation roles used for Bank Rate, CPIH, unemployment and real GDP. Each input keeps its observation ID and version, source-registry version, measured period, value and knowledge time.

MGI applies the published thresholds to those fixed inputs. It hashes the complete manifest and results to create the snapshot ID. Reproduction succeeds only when the recalculated results and content-derived ID match. The public page reads this checked repository record; the optional database stores the same snapshot with foreign keys to the exact observation versions.

26. Product boundary

MGI provides general financial research and education. Market states, forecasts, scores, twins and mechanism maps are not personal recommendations, trade signals or instructions.

27. Correction rule

A material correction gets its own reference and publication version. The correction says what changed, why and where to find the original. An empty correction list does not prove that no errors exist.

28. Governance and data footprint

The website has no user accounts, MGI analytics, marketing trackers, browser storage, MGI contact forms or non-essential cookies. GitHub hosts the site and the public feedback forms.

These governance pages are implementation drafts. They have not received professional legal review.

29. Public interface and domain state

The homepage places the four current readings in the founder-selected signal-field layout. It uses the same checked datasets and fixed labels as the Market Pulse page. Source condition is shown with words as well as colour.

The social preview image was produced with an image-generation tool and checked manually. It contains indicator names but no economic values, dates or analytical claims.

marketgeneralintelligence.co.uk is the intended canonical domain and marketgeneralintelligence.com is reserved for a route-preserving redirect. Neither is described as active until DNS, HTTPS and redirects are verified. The current metadata falls back to the recorded GitHub Pages address unless a validated deployment provides NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL.
