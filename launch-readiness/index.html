Launch status / reviewed 2026-07-29

What works now, and what still blocks a wider launch.

MGI is a working free public beta. This page lists what is available today and what must be completed before it is promoted more widely.

Available now

Working today

  • Four official UK economic states with independent source health
  • Deterministic calculations and safe value withholding
  • Public methodology, source register, release history and corrections
  • Immutable Daily Brief, event record and open forecast
  • No accounts, payments, advertising, marketing trackers or non-essential cookies
  • No paid model request on a public page view

Limits already enforced

Rules MGI will not cross

  • General research and education only
  • No personal recommendations, ratings or transaction instructions
  • No broker links, order execution, position sizing or portfolio allocation
  • Forecast uncertainty and original probabilities remain visible
  • Professional legal clearance is not claimed
  • Material expansion near the UK regulatory perimeter is blocked pending specialist review

Before wider promotion

Still required

  • Confirm the legal operator and a suitable public service address
  • Obtain qualified UK financial-services and privacy review
  • Verify production hosting, DNS, HTTPS and route-preserving redirects
  • Update this notice if the host, cookies, analytics or forms change
  • Complete company, tax, consumer terms and support gates before payment
  • Do not resolve the 30 July 2026 forecast without authoritative evidence
